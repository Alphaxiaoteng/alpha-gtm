# PLEIAD Brand Guidelines

## Brand architecture

| Layer | Name | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Public product brand** | **PLEIAD** | The customer-facing omnichannel operations product |
| **Descriptor** | AI-native omnichannel operations | Explains the category without reducing the product to writing |
| **Internal repository/runtime** | Alpha-Nexus EgoLite | Retained until a separate, tested code-renaming change |
| **Browser runtime** | EgoLite | A dependency/runtime, not the public product brand |

The name PLEIAD evokes a coordinated group: many channels, accounts, agents, and commercial actions remaining individually visible while operating as one system. Do not turn the metaphor into cosmic decoration or a star-logo cliché.

## Positioning

**English category:** AI-native omnichannel operations platform.

**Chinese category:** AI 原生全渠道商业运营平台。

**Positioning statement:** PLEIAD is designed to connect content, products, accounts, stores, and browser agents into a controlled operating loop from preparation and approval to publishing, verification, and commercial attribution.

**Core promise:** Prepare once. Operate everywhere. Prove every result.

**Chinese promise:** 一次准备，全域运营，结果可证。

## Message hierarchy

1. **Outcome:** Let one operator safely run more channels, products, and accounts.
2. **Mechanism:** Shared source facts, platform adaptation, frozen plans, explicit approval, controlled execution, and evidence.
3. **Trust:** Local-first, human-in-the-loop, recoverable, and auditable.
4. **Commercial value:** Connect discovery and publishing to clicks, orders, refunds, and margin.

### Homepage copy

**H1:** Let AI operate across channels—without giving up control.

**Chinese H1:** 让 AI 接管跨平台运营，但不交出控制权。

**Supporting copy:** Manage content, products, accounts, stores, and browser agents in one local-first control loop. Prepare in batches, approve irreversible actions, recover from platform friction, and verify every result.

## Voice

- Calm, precise, and operational.
- State what happened, what is still pending, and what evidence exists.
- Prefer concrete verbs: prepare, freeze, approve, submit, verify, recover, attribute.
- Explain limits without apology or marketing fog.

### Avoid

- “AI writing tool” as the category.
- “One-click viral”, “fully autonomous everywhere”, “unattended money machine”.
- Calling preparation, upload, or a toast message “published”.
- Claiming every listed platform has verified live submission.
- Generic cosmic prose, star fields, purple gradients, or an overly literal constellation identity.

## Visual direction

- **Base:** near-black, warm white, restrained neutral gray.
- **Signal accent:** one warm copper/orange used only for active intent and controlled execution.
- **Typography:** neutral grotesk for product communication; a restrained serif may appear in editorial headlines only.
- **Motion:** state transitions should communicate orchestration—nodes becoming ready, approval propagating, and evidence returning—not decorative orbiting.
- **Logo:** wordmark-first. A symbol may express several independent units forming one controlled field, but must not resemble a cross, generic four-point star, or crypto mark.

## Claims boundary

- Label public capabilities as `available`, `experimental`, `manual handoff`, `unverified`, or `roadmap`.
- Platform catalog coverage is not proof of live publishing.
- Current unverified or manual-handoff capabilities must remain labeled.
- Revenue metrics require a traceable event chain; projected revenue is not realized revenue.
- PLEIAD is local-first software, not a public cloud account dashboard.

## Public identity

- **Canonical domain assignment:** `pleiadops.com`
- **Status:** domain selection complete; registration and legal/trademark clearance pending.
- Use `PLEIAD` in the product wordmark and `pleiadops.com` in URLs and handles.
