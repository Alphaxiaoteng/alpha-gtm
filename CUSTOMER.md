# PLEIAD Ideal Customer Profile

> **Guiding principle:** Serve the operator who is personally accountable for many valuable accounts, channels, products, approvals, and outcomes.

## The ICP: the content-commerce matrix operator

The ICP is **not a generic creator looking for an AI writer**. It is the owner-operator or operations lead in a 3-20 person content-commerce team who manages multiple brands, stores, or account matrices from one controlled workstation.

| Audience | Typical situation | Response to PLEIAD |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary: content-commerce operations lead** | 2-10 brands/stores, 5-30 media accounts, launches and campaigns across several platforms | Values one controlled execution loop with explicit approval, manual takeover, recovery, and evidence |
| **Secondary: agency/MCN execution seat** | One operator owns a defined set of client accounts while approval still happens in Feishu, WeCom, or email | Uses PLEIAD as the local execution and evidence layer, not a cloud collaboration suite |
| **Anti-persona: casual single-channel creator** | One account, occasional posting, low operational risk | Native platform tools are usually enough |

## Current state and triggers

**What they do today:**

- Plan in spreadsheets, Feishu, Notion, or chat.
- Generate and edit material across separate AI, design, and storage tools.
- Copy content and product facts into each creator center or store backend.
- Use multiple browser profiles or shared accounts, then return links and screenshots manually.
- Reconstruct who approved what and whether it really published from fragmented records.

**What triggers active search:**

- Account count grows beyond what one person can safely track.
- A launch or promotion requires coordinated media and store updates.
- A wrong-account post, missed approval, stale price, or unverifiable publish creates an incident.
- AI increases output volume while the operator must retain final control.
- A client or manager asks for an audit trail from source to result.

## Profile data

**Primary psychographic:** They want leverage, not loss of control. They accept automation when its inputs, approval boundary, execution state, and evidence are visible.

**Observable ICP signals:**

- Already operates several platform backends every week.
- Uses a repeatable campaign or launch process.
- Cares about account identity, product truth, and approval state.
- Has experienced duplicate work, wrong-account risk, or missing proof.
- Measures time saved, throughput, conversion, orders, or contribution margin.

**Secondary audience:** Local-first technical creators and small brand teams that need browser-agent execution with human takeover.

**Out of scope:** Cloud-first enterprises requiring immediate SSO/RBAC, buyers seeking CAPTCHA or 2FA bypass, and users expecting every platform to be autonomously published without approval.

## Pain points in the customer's voice

1. **“同一批内容和商品信息，我要在十几个后台反复填。”**
2. **“最怕不是慢，而是发错账号、发错价格，或者重复点提交。”**
3. **“AI 可以准备，但最终发什么、发到哪里必须让我确认。”**
4. **“出了验证码或页面变化，系统要停下来把控制权交还给我。”**
5. **“发完不能只说成功，我需要链接、状态、截图和后续订单证据。”**

## What the ICP values

| Value | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Visible control** | Valuable accounts and irreversible actions cannot depend on opaque automation |
| **Batch throughput** | The business value appears when one operator handles many channels and products |
| **Local ownership** | Account context, assets, and evidence stay under the operator's control |
| **Recovery** | Platform changes and login checkpoints are normal, not exceptional |
| **Commercial traceability** | Publishing activity must connect to clicks, orders, refunds, and margin |

## Goals and success

- **Primary:** Reduce human time per verified cross-platform campaign.
- **Secondary:** Increase the number of channels and products one operator can safely manage.
- **Tertiary:** Improve AI-search visibility and commercial attribution without mass-producing duplicate content.

**North-star metric:** Verified commercial value per hour of operator attention.

## Hypotheses to validate

- Conduct 10-15 interviews with content-commerce operators before treating segment size or willingness to pay as confirmed.
- Validate whether local-first control is a paid differentiator or primarily a trust enabler.
- Validate the first commerce connector from real customer store usage; do not infer it from platform popularity alone.

## Quick reference

**One-line ICP:** The person accountable for coordinating content, products, accounts, approvals, execution, and proof across a content-commerce matrix.

**Product decision checklist:**

- Does this reduce operator attention without hiding state?
- Does it preserve explicit approval for irreversible actions?
- Does it work in batches and recover from partial failure?
- Does it produce evidence that can be tied to a commercial result?
- Are unsupported platform capabilities clearly marked unverified?
