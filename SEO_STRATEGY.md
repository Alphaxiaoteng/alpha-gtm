## Traditional SEO foundation

### Technical requirements

- Serve stable, accessible HTML with unique title, description, H1, canonical URL, and update date.
- Publish `robots.txt`, XML sitemap, RSS/Atom, useful internal links, and a real 404 page.
- Redirect `www` permanently to the apex domain.
- Keep public marketing infrastructure separate from Electron, SQLite, EgoLite, and local HTTP endpoints.
- Target fast mobile rendering and avoid hiding core facts behind client-only interactions.

### Structured data

1. `Organization` on the about/entity layer.
2. `SoftwareApplication` on the product page.
3. `Article` and `BreadcrumbList` on research and education pages.
4. `Product` + `Offer` on actual purchasable product pages only.
5. `ProductGroup` for apparel or other variants with stable URLs.

Structured data must match visible content. Do not add fake ratings, FAQ markup for invisible answers, or unsupported platform claims.

### Entity consistency

Use exactly:

- **Name:** PLEIAD
- **Category:** AI-native omnichannel operations platform
- **Chinese category:** AI 原生全渠道商业运营平台
- **Canonical URL:** `https://pleiadops.com`
- **Core promise:** Prepare once. Operate everywhere. Prove every result.

Keep company name, founder, product definition, logo, social handles, and `sameAs` links consistent across the website and public profiles.

## GEO by platform

### Google Search, AI Overviews, and Gemini

Google documents no separate AI Overview submission, special schema, or AI-only text file. Follow Search Essentials: useful original content, indexable pages, visible text, matching structured data, internal links, and sound page experience.

Primary reference: <https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features>

### ChatGPT Search and Shopping

- Allow `OAI-SearchBot` on public pages and verify the CDN/WAF does not return a challenge or 403.
- Make direct answers, limitations, dates, authors, and source evidence visible in HTML.
- Track observable ChatGPT referrals separately.
- For commerce, keep product pages, price, inventory, images, shipping, and returns current. Shopify Catalog or an approved direct OpenAI product feed may improve data freshness; neither guarantees ranking.

References: <https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq> and <https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-improved-shopping-results-from-chatgpt-search>

### Perplexity

Allow `PerplexityBot` for public pages intended to appear in its search results. Pages should return stable 200 responses with a complete answer, clear source identity, and durable URLs.

Reference: <https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers>

### Bing and IndexNow

Verify the site in Bing Webmaster Tools, submit the sitemap, and notify significant additions, updates, and deletions through IndexNow. A successful IndexNow response confirms receipt, not indexing.

Reference: <https://www.indexnow.org/documentation>

### Claude

No official Claude search-citation webmaster program was identified. Treat `ClaudeBot` access as a separate model-training policy decision and do not promise Claude citation visibility from crawler permission.

## Query fan-out content format

AI search systems may rewrite a broad question into definition, comparison, evidence, freshness, implementation, risk, and alternative queries. Each important page should therefore include:

1. A 60-120 word direct answer near the top.
2. Clear “for whom / not for whom” boundaries.
3. A workflow, table, or concrete example.
4. Real limitations and current verification state.
5. Original evidence, method, author, date, and last update.
6. Relevant product, use-case, documentation, and comparison links.

Do not create hundreds of near-identical pages to mechanically cover question variants.

## Media and commerce distribution

The website page is the canonical source. PLEIAD then creates a platform package with stable facts and platform-specific hooks, lengths, media, and calls to action.

```text
canonical source
  -> media adaptations
  -> commerce listing/feed adaptations
  -> frozen preflight
  -> explicit approval
  -> controlled publish/manual takeover
  -> URL/status/screenshot evidence
  -> traffic/order/refund/margin attribution
```

Do not syndicate identical long-form copy. Link back to the canonical page where the platform allows it, preserve campaign IDs, and keep commercial disclosures visible.

## Commerce SEO and AI-shopping foundation

- Maintain one product truth object for SPU/SKU, brand, title, description, variant, image, price, currency, availability, shipping, returns, and canonical URL.
- Synchronize that object to visible product pages, JSON-LD, Merchant Center, approved AI product feeds, and store connectors.
- Use stable variant URLs and accurate product identifiers.
- Treat feed acceptance and crawler access as eligibility, not guaranteed ranking or inclusion.

Google product reference: <https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product>

## Measurement

### Search and GEO

- Indexed/crawlable canonical pages.
- Non-brand impressions and qualified organic conversions.
- PLEIAD definition accuracy across a fixed benchmark set of questions.
- Citation share, cited URLs, brand co-mentions, and observable AI referrals.

### Distribution and commerce

- Verified publish success, manual takeover, unknown-result, and duplicate-prevention rates.
- `source_page_id -> campaign_id -> publish_job_id -> platform_post_id -> click_id -> order_id -> refund_id` coverage.
- Revenue, refund-adjusted contribution margin, and operator time per verified campaign.

Run the same 30-50 benchmark questions monthly across major search/AI products, storing the answer date, cited URLs, brand mentions, factual errors, and referrals. Follow each service's terms; do not bypass login or anti-bot controls.

## Anti-patterns

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Positioning as an AI writer | Position as a controlled omnichannel operations platform |
| Hundreds of thin AI-generated pages | Fewer original pages with evidence and clear decisions |
| Identical copy on every platform | Stable facts plus native platform adaptation |
| Hidden prompts or crawler-only text | The same useful visible content for people and crawlers |
| Feed/schema/page price drift | One product truth synchronized everywhere |
| Calling upload or submit “published” | Independent platform evidence and explicit status |
| Claiming AI citation guarantees | Eligibility, monitoring, and measurable citation share |
