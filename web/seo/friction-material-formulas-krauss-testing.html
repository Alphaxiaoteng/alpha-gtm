Direct Engineering Summary

Over 20 years of commercial vehicle friction R&D, Zhejiang Kooying Autoparts has developed 3 core formulation series and 30+ asbestos-free compounds in partnership with the National University of Defense Technology and China University of Geosciences (Wuhan). Tested across two European Krauss rigs, a North American Chase tester, and four constant-speed machines, KOOYING maintains μ 0.35–0.38 with 0.040 mm wear at 400°C+—under half the 0.085 mm wear of typical aftermarket references.
